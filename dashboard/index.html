Source: St. Louis Metropolitan Police Department (SLMPD) crime data as posted by the City of St. Louis, which publishes each month's data around the 5th of the following month. An "offense" is one row in that data; an "incident" is one police report, which can contain several offenses. Offenses are grouped by the NIBRS "crimes against" classification. Locations are the addresses in the data, usually block-level, and a few records without a usable location are left off the map. Neighborhoods come from the City's data.

Address search: the address you type is sent to the City of St. Louis, OpenStreetMap and U.S. Census Bureau geocoding services to find its location. This site does not store it. A pin you drop, or your device's location if you share it, is used only in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
