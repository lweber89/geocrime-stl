A hotspot is where offenses were reported, not where danger is. More Busy places such as shopping areas, downtown and hospitals appear more often because many people pass through and report there. Counts are not adjusted for how many people live or visit. Sensitive offenses are counted, but their exact location is withheld, so they never appear as a street in the Blocks list. See the methodology page for details.
Source: City of St. Louis (SLMPD) monthly crime data. Rates use 2020 census populations. Dashboard design adapted from a project by Reddit user u/autosoap.
